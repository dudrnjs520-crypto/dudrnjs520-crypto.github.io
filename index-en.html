Convoy Merge
Defense

The highway already belongs to the zombie horde. What is left is a single armored truck. Load six squad slots in the cargo bed, merge matching squad members to evolve them, and hold off the zombies chasing you from behind with rear-facing fire until you reach your destination. A casual merge defense you play in portrait, with one hand.

Google PlayIn preparation for launch · Free (with ads and in-app purchases)

01 About the Game

MERGE

Six cargo slots, evolve by merging

Drop a squad member onto another of the same class and level and it evolves one step (up to Lv.6). Five classes — pistol shooter, flamethrower, tesla engineer, sniper and mortar — plus two hidden combinations.

FIRE

Rear-facing fire from the truck

Squad members fire from the cargo bed at the road chasing behind. Keep the combo going and Fever raises your attack speed.

MODES

Four modes

A solo run through ten chapters, two-lane co-op defending two lanes with an AI partner, an endless ∞ Endless Run, and a weekly challenge whose rules change every week.

ADS

Ads only when you want them

No forced ads. All six rewarded ad spots play only when you tap them, and with the permanent ad-removal entitlement you get the same rewards straight away.
