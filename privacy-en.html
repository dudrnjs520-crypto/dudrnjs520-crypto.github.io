Privacy Policy

Service Convoy Merge Defense (Android app; Korean title 달리는 트럭 디펜스)
Operator Beonyeonggyeol (번영결), represented by Cho Yeong-gwon

Beonyeonggyeol ("we", "us" or "the Operator") provides the mobile game Convoy Merge Defense ("the Game") and processes your personal information only to the extent necessary to run the Game. This policy describes plainly what information the Game keeps, where it is kept, and what we do not receive.

1. Scope and Basic Principles

  • This policy applies to the Android app Convoy Merge Defense distributed through Google Play and to its updates.
  • The Game has no sign-up process. We do not ask you for your name, phone number, address, date of birth, contacts, photos or any other information that could identify you.
  • Game progress is by default stored only on your device and is not sent to our servers.
  • The only information sent to our servers is (1) the nickname, player identifier and record you submit when you upload an Endless Run result, (2) the room code, nickname and real-time combat and deployment state exchanged during Online Co-op (real-time two-player co-op), and (3) information that Google's services automatically exchange for advertising, consent and purchases.
  • Nicknames shown on the leaderboard pass through a filter that screens out profanity, derogatory words and words impersonating the Operator; inappropriate names are hidden or replaced with a name generated automatically on your device. If you choose "Report" on a leaderboard entry, the Operator reviews it and takes action (see 2-2-1).
  • Information exchanged in Online Co-op is relayed only briefly, and only so that it reaches the other player in the same room; it is not retained on the server after the match ends (no permanent storage).
  • We do not receive payment-method information such as card or bank account numbers. Payments are handled by Google Play.

2. Information We Process and How We Process It

2-1. Information stored only on your device (not sent to servers)

  • Game progress (chapter progress, squad members and cards you own, currencies such as diamonds and gold, upgrade levels), game settings, best records and the items you have purchased
  • The nickname and player identifier used for the leaderboard (see 2-2), and whether you own the permanent ad-removal entitlement
  • This information stays in the app's storage on your device, and the Operator cannot read it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or reset your device.

When you set a new personal best in Endless Run, the information below is sent over the network to our server and published to other players.

  • If you change your nickname, records you uploaded under the previous nickname remain on the server. To have them deleted, follow the procedure in Section 6.

2-3. Online Co-op (real-time two-player co-op)

In Online Co-op, where two players who join the same room split two lanes and fight together, the information below is passed through the server to the other player in the same room only while the match is running.

ItemDetails
Room code A random four-digit number generated when a room is created. Used only to pair the two players in the same room
Room password An optional value of up to four digits, set only if you want one. Used only by the host's app to verify a player joining the room
Nickname The same nickname as on the leaderboard (a name generated automatically on your device if you have not entered one). Shown to the other player in the same room
Session identifier A random string (12 letters and digits) generated on your device when you join a room. Used only to tell you apart from the other player in the same room; it is different from the player identifier used for the leaderboard
Real-time combat and deployment state The state of the match in progress: the current wave, and the position and health of remaining zombies, the health, gold and upgrade levels of both trucks, squad member slots together with aiming, firing and special abilities, skill and fever state, distance travelled, and so on
Your squad information Squad member upgrade tiers and card grades (used to balance the opposing truck's attack and health)
Relay path A one-off channel of Supabase Realtime (Seoul region). Nothing is written to the database
Retention Relayed only while the match and the room are active; it is not retained on the server once the match ends or you leave the room (no permanent storage)
  • Share the room code and password only with people you know. Online Co-op uses a public broadcast channel, and the room password is only a check performed by the host's app.
  • Your nickname is shown as it is to the other player in the same room. Do not include your real name, phone number, email address, home address or other information that could identify you.
  • If you do not use Online Co-op and play alone (or with an AI partner), none of this information is transmitted at all.
  • Match results are not stored on the server as permanent records and are not counted toward the Endless Run leaderboard. Chapters cleared in co-op remain only on each player's device.

2-4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game contains only rewarded ads, which play only when you tap the button yourself. The Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects the following information in order to serve ads and shares it with Google.

  • Advertising ID (ADID, the Android advertising identifier) and app set ID
  • Device and app information: in-app interactions such as app launches and screen views, app version and operating system information
  • Diagnostic information: performance-related information such as launch time, hang rate and energy usage
  • Approximate location estimated from your IP address (city level; not precise location)

The purposes are ad delivery, frequency capping, invalid-click and fraud prevention, and ad performance measurement. The Operator does not separately store or read this information.

2-5. Ad consent management (Google UMP)

The Game uses Google's consent management tool (User Messaging Platform). In regions where consent is required by law (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, for example), a consent screen for ad personalization is shown on first launch, and the choice you make (consent, refusal or detailed settings) and the device information needed to remember that choice are stored by Google. You can change your consent at any time in the app's ad consent settings or in your Android settings.

2-6. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases of paid items such as diamonds, Starter Packs and the permanent ad-removal entitlement are processed by the Google Play billing system. We see only the order number, product ID and purchase token (used to confirm whether an item has been granted and to prevent duplicate grants). We do not collect or store payment-method information such as card numbers, bank account numbers or billing addresses.

2-7. Inquiries and reports by email

If you contact us by email or report an entry from the leaderboard, the sender's address, the body of the email (for a report email, the reported player's nickname, player identifier and record) and any attachments remain in the Operator's mailbox. We use this information only to respond to inquiries, handle reports and resolve disputes.

3. Purposes of Processing Personal Information

  1. Providing the Game and maintaining game progress
  2. Storing and displaying Endless Run rankings (the leaderboard) and preventing manipulation of rankings
  3. Checking public nicknames against the prohibited-word filter and hiding them where necessary, and receiving and handling reports of inappropriate nicknames and leaderboard records
  4. Relaying the room code, nickname and combat state to the other player in Online Co-op (real-time two-player co-op) so that two players can play together
  5. Delivering rewarded ads, granting their rewards and measuring ad performance
  6. Confirming in-app purchases, granting items and preventing duplicate grants
  7. Responding to user inquiries and resolving disputes
  8. Identifying and restricting abnormal use (such as manipulated records)
  9. Complying with legal obligations

6. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

  • You may at any time request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of the processing of your personal information.
  • Please send requests to dudrnjs520@gmail.com. Including the nickname and record shown on your in-game leaderboard screen (or your player identifier) will help us find your data faster. We will inform you of the outcome within three business days of receiving your request.
  • If you have changed your nickname, records uploaded under a previous nickname may still remain; please include the previous nickname if you want them deleted as well.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it and turn off personalized ads in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Withdrawing ad consent: you can do so in the app's ad consent settings (Google UMP) or by contacting us at the email address above.
  • You can report another player's inappropriate nickname with ⋮ → "Report" on the leaderboard, or hide it on your device with "Hide only".
  • If your nickname was replaced with an automatically generated name by the prohibited-word filter, you can set a new one free of charge in the app, and you can request deletion of your own records through the contact email above.
  • Information relayed to the other player during Online Co-op is not stored on the server, so no record of it remains to be deleted.
  • Requests may also be made through a legal guardian or an authorized representative.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint about our processing with your local data protection authority.

7. Advertising Identifiers and Personalized Advertising

The Game does not use cookies. Advertising is provided through Google AdMob, and personalized ads use the advertising ID (ADID). If you do not want personalized ads, you can turn them off in the settings described in Section 6; ads will still be shown, but they will be less relevant to you. If you prefer not to see ads at all, you can purchase the permanent ad-removal entitlement as an in-app purchase.

8. Personal Information of Children Under 14

  • The Game is not primarily directed at children under 14 (the age set by the Korean Personal Information Protection Act), and we do not serve personalized advertising to children.
  • A child under 14 may use the Game only with the consent of their legal guardian.
  • If we become aware that information of a child under 14 (for example, a leaderboard nickname or record) has been collected, we will delete it without delay. A legal guardian may request access to or deletion of that information at the email address above.

9. Measures to Keep Personal Information Secure

  • Information exchanged with our servers is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • The leaderboard database applies row-level security: reading and inserting are permitted, while permission to modify or delete is not given to the app. Inappropriate leaderboard rows that are reported are deleted by the Operator after review.
  • Public nicknames are screened by a prohibited-word filter; inappropriate names are displayed as "Hidden Name", and reports from users are handled by the Operator.
  • Online Co-op relays information on a one-off channel per room and writes nothing to the database. Share the room code and password only with people you know.
  • The app contains only public keys; keys with administrative privileges are never included in the app.
  • We limit the items we collect to the minimum necessary to operate the Game.
